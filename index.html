countermeasures

Execute in order. The first three take under ten minutes total.

01

Rotate credentials

Start with the breached account, then anywhere you reused that password. Reuse turns one breach into ten.

02

Enable 2FA

Use an authenticator app or hardware key over SMS. It blocks most takeovers even when the password leaks.

03

Deploy a vault

Unique, random passwords per site — you only memorize one strong master passphrase.

04

Assume phishing

Leaked addresses attract targeted scams. Treat unexpected login alerts and invoices as hostile until verified.

05

Freeze credit

If IDs or financial data leaked, a freeze with the major bureaus blocks new accounts in your name.

06

Purge dead accounts

Delete services you no longer use. An account that doesn't exist cannot be breached.
